Why a membership, not a course

A course gets paid for once. People watch a couple of videos, implement one or two things, and stop, no accountability, no way back to more revenue without selling them something else from scratch. A membership solves both problems with the same content.

Your content split into separate courses by area of the game, not one block. Reads as more value, gives room to add more later
The retention driver
One live group Q&A call a month, an hour, open questions. Keeps people renewing, and doubles as new content for the vault
